# Wireframes and Storyboards

This storyboard converts my outline and sketches from Part I into screen-by-screen wireframes that show how a reader will move through the story. Each "screen" represents a Shorthand chapter or scroll section.

All wireframes include:
- Titles
- Expected chart placeholders  
- Annotations/captions
- Flow of text
- How visuals support the story arc

## Visual Hierarchy Notes

1. **Hero visualizations** (Sections 2 & 5) get the most screen space
2. **Supporting charts** (Sections 3 & 4) use small multiples for efficiency
3. **Text is secondary** to visuals but provides essential context
4. **Annotations** guide interpretation at key moments
5. **Color coding**: Red for decline, gray for current state, blue for past
