# Visualizing Government Debt

This page presents my analysis and visualization of **OECD General Government Debt (% of GDP)** using **Tableau**.

## 🧭 Overview
The visualization compares government debt-to-GDP ratios across OECD countries from 1995–2019.  
It highlights fiscal trends across time and identifies which countries have debt levels above or below their GDP.  
The goal is to show both **magnitude** and **pattern** — helping viewers grasp long-term fiscal pressures among OECD nations.
